Estimation method

This tool uses a practical browser-side approximation, not a model tokenizer. English text is estimated at about four characters per token, with adjustments for CJK characters, numbers, symbols, and whitespace. Always verify mission-critical billing against your provider dashboard.

Ways to use this calculator

  • Estimate the cost of summarizing many articles, tickets, or support conversations.
  • Compare short prompts against long prompts before building an AI workflow.
  • Plan monthly spend for batch jobs by increasing the request count.
  • Use custom rates when your provider publishes different pricing.
