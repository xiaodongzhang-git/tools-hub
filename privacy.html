Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-01-01

Tools Hub is committed to protecting your privacy. This website is designed so that all processing—including text conversion, encoding, decoding, and calculations—happens locally in your browser. We do not upload, store, or share the data you enter into any tool.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not store any text, images, or data you input.
  • We do not use a database, server storage, or cloud storage.
  • We do not collect personal information such as names, emails, IP addresses, or device identifiers.

Cookies and Analytics

Tools Hub does not use cookies to track users. Limited, anonymous data may be collected by third-party services such as Google AdSense or Google Analytics if you enable them. These services operate under their own privacy policies.

Third-Party Advertisements

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google may use cookies or other tracking technologies to personalize ads. You can review Google's policies here: Google Privacy Policy.

Local Storage

Some tools (such as Text Notes) may store data locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time by deleting your browser data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as new tools or features are added. Updates will always be posted on this page for transparency.
