How to Use

            
    
              
  1. Paste a full JWT token into the input box above (e.g. xxxxx.yyyyy.zzzzz).
    2. 
              
  2. Click Decode to parse and display the header and payload as formatted JSON.
    3. 
              
  3. The signature part is shown as raw Base64URL text.
    4. 
            


            

              This tool only decodes JWTs. It does not verify the signature or the
              token's validity. Do not treat decoded data as trusted without server-side validation.
            

            

              All decoding is performed locally in your browser. Your JWTs are not transmitted to any
              server.
